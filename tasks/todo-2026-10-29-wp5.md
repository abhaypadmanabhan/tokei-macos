## Decoder shape
Byte input reads bounded blocks from Data or a file. Forward bits decode table headers; reverse bits decode Huffman/FSE streams. Per-frame tables and three recent offsets reset at every frame. Output is delivered one <=128 KiB block at a time; a bounded history retains only the window for backreferences. Known content sizes and checksums are checked before success. Errors throw; no process exits, unchecked shifts, force unwraps or dictionary fallback.
