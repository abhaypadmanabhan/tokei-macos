## Decisions, constraints, risk and rollback

- Preserve the existing highest-record Claude correction and account-allocation rules.
  Missing models retain their tokens as `unknown`; synthetic usage contributes nothing.
- Use bounded current-month maps and rebuild them on calendar/month changes. Use exact
  public rates plus seed overrides; reject guessed family prices and incomplete rates.
- Retain partial priced dollars with a coverage flag in Maxxer and a warning in Codex cost.
  Keep all-unknown value unavailable. Snapshot shapes remain frozen.
- Added constraints: failing regression tests for model switches, cache resumes, account
  dedupe/correction, synthetic exclusion, month boundaries, exact pricing, rate-cache
  replacement, MTD component arithmetic and unknown coverage.
- Risk: medium for local parser/cache and displayed-value changes; no remote resource or
  quota lifecycle change. Rollback is to revert `e72a598`, `ef10d5b`, then `fbfad6a` locally.
- Not verified: final full-suite rerun, comparative base CPU/RAM, app/UI behavior, independent
  cross-model review. The orchestrator must resolve the Claude baseline policy before the
  0.5% acceptance criterion can pass. Dev/main and push were not used.
- Learn: small output-token corrections can cause a material dollar gap even when total
  tokens nearly agree; price the components before aggregating dollars. Cache state includes
  model context, not just byte offsets. Daily model totals remove MTD scaling approximations.
