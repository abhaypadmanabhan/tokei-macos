## Outcome

Tokei 0.14.0 is out and offered as an in-app update. It shows the SuperGrok usage pool, counted once when Grok and
Cursor share it. People can now connect Copilot and OpenCode Go through their official usage APIs with a key they
paste; both are off until turned on, and the keys stay in the Mac's Keychain. Warnings about unreadable logs no longer
include folder paths.

## Detail
